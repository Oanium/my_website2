Nov. 20, 2026

  So recently I've learning how to make games using love2d. Going through tutorials and trying to get into the GD50 was good, but lecturing will never be quite as effective as making something yourself. So I went on and decided to make my own game; a calculator. And so I did, over the course of a few days I made my own calculator.


My "Cal-Cu-Lator" app

  One thing that I really enjoyed about this project was the fact that my reliance on AI was limited, like I only used it once I think, and it even with that, I eventually replaced the code that it generated with my own is such a satisfying feeling. Right now I feel really confident in my ability to program and finally seeing the fruits of my labor is such an awesome sight to see. Before this project I felt that I hadn't really learned much from college, learning C and C++ was fun, but results weren't quick, so I switched to lua, learned love2d, and finally built something that can stand on its own two legs. I know that this won't impress any employers, but I'm well impressed by my own work.
